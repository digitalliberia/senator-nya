Humanitarian Service • Nimba County

Nya D. Twayen Foundation

Humanitarian Service for the People of Nimba — putting compassion into action, village by village, family by family.

The Nya D. Twayen Foundation is the humanitarian arm dedicated to social welfare, relief, and uplift across Nimba County. From medical donations and educational scholarships to widow support and youth empowerment, the Foundation puts compassion into action — village by village, family by family.
