Honorable Nya D. Twayen, Jr | Senator, Nimba County

A voice for NimbaA Servant of the People

Honorable Senator Nya D. Twayen, Jr. serves the people of Nimba county in the Liberian Senate with dedication, integrity and a deep commitment to grassroots development. A son of the soil with strong roots in his community, the senator has devoted his career to advancing the welfare of every citizen – from the rural farmer in Saclepea to entrepreneur in Sanniquellie and students across the region.

Through legislative advocacy, humanitarian programs and the work of the Nya D. Twayen Foundation, he is building a more inclusive, prosperous and dignified future for all Nimbaians and Liberians.
